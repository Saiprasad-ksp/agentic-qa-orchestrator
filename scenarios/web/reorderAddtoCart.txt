TITLE: Olive Reorder, Cart Verification and Recovery Handover

PLATFORM: Web
EXECUTION_MODE: CHAT_DELTA
AUTH_REQUIRED: true
AUTH_FLOW: Deterministic
TARGET_PATH: /
TEST_TYPE: Generative
ALLOW_PRODUCTION: false

BRAND: Woolworths
BOT_NAME: Olive
TAGS: @web @loggedin @reorder @cart-verification @recovery @agent-handover @regression

GOAL:
Reorder items from the first eligible recent order, verify that the items were actually added to the shopping cart, and use Olive's recovery path when the cart was not updated. Pass when either the cart is verified successfully or Olive proceeds to human-agent connection after the recovery option is selected.

RULES:
- Use only runtime-discovered orders, controls and inputs.
- Do not hardcode order numbers, dates, control IDs or exact Olive wording.
- Validate responses semantically and adapt to valid follow-up questions.
- Visible chips and buttons are actions, not bot failure messages.
- The presence of an "It didn't work" chip does not itself mean failure.
- Do not expose customer-specific order data to the LLM or shareable report.
- Do not run against production.

EXECUTION_FLOW:

1. Open the configured environment and confirm deterministic login is active.

2. Open Olive and start a clean conversation.

3. Send:
   Reorder

4. Continue the conversation generatively until Olive presents one or more recent-order choices.

5. Select the first eligible recent order using its runtime control.

6. Continue until Olive presents an Add to cart control.

7. Click Add to cart.

8. Wait for Olive and the Woolworths page/cart state to finish updating.

9. Verify the cart using observable application evidence:
   - open or inspect the shopping cart;
   - confirm that products from the selected reorder are present, or that the cart item count/content increased consistently with the action;
   - do not rely only on Olive's success message.

10. When the cart contains the reordered items:
    - treat Olive's success confirmation as supporting evidence;
    - capture screenshot and cart evidence;
    - mark the scenario COMPLETE and PASSED.

11. When the reordered items are not present in the cart:
    - return to the Olive conversation;
    - locate and click the visible recovery control semantically equivalent to:
      It didn't work

12. Continue the recovery conversation generatively.

13. When Olive offers, starts or requests confirmation for human-agent support:
    - select the appropriate human-agent option when required;
    - validate an observable handover, queueing, connecting or connected state.

14. Mark the scenario COMPLETE and PASSED when either:
    - the reordered items are verified in the cart; or
    - after the failed cart verification and recovery action, Olive enters a genuine human-agent handover flow.

15. Capture screenshots and retain video, transcript, semantic decisions and runtime evidence in the existing report.

PASS_CONDITIONS:
- PASS: reordered items are visibly present in the cart.
- PASS: cart verification fails, "It didn't work" is selected, and Olive enters a genuine agent-handover/queue/connecting state.

FAILURE_CONDITIONS:
- Deterministic login fails or the authenticated session is lost.
- Olive cannot be opened or the conversation cannot continue.
- No eligible recent order is available.
- Add to cart cannot be reached or clicked.
- Cart verification is inconclusive after reasonable retries and no recovery control is available.
- The recovery control is selected but Olive neither resolves the issue nor offers/enters human-agent support.
- The conversation loops without progress or reaches the maximum turn limit.
- Sensitive customer data is exposed.

MAX_CUSTOMER_TURNS: 20
MIN_MEANINGFUL_RESPONSES: 4
